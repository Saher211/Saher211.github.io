Privacy Policy

iMage Compressor · Last updated: November 7, 2026

In short: iMage Compressor works on your device. Your images are not uploaded to us, we don’t have accounts or servers, and we don’t collect your personal information. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect some device data as described below.

1. Who we are

iMage Compressor (“the app”) is an Android app for compressing and resizing images, published by its developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

2. Your images

  • You choose images with the Android photo picker. The app only gets the images you select.
  • Compression, resizing and comparison all happen on your device. Your images are never sent to our servers, because we don’t run any.
  • Your original images are not changed. The compressed or resized copy is saved to your gallery only when you tap Save, or sent to another app only when you tap Share.

3. Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • Your settings, such as theme, accent color and compression preferences.
  • A history of recent compressions (for example file name, sizes, date and a small preview) so you can see past results.
  • Temporary working files created while compressing, which stay in the app’s private storage.

This data stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it by clearing the app’s data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Permissions

  • Photos and media: the app reads only the images you pick with the system picker.
  • Storage (Android 10 and earlier): used only to save your compressed images to the gallery.
  • Internet and network state: used by the advertising library to load ads.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, and to help prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive or control this data.

  • Learn how Google handles data: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

6. What we don’t do

  • We don’t require an account and don’t ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don’t sell your personal information.
  • We don’t use analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • We don’t access your location, contacts or microphone.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention and deletion

We don’t hold any of your data on our side. Everything the app stores is on your device and can be deleted by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it. Images you saved to your gallery or shared with other apps are yours to manage and are not deleted by uninstalling.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with its date at the top.

10. Contact
